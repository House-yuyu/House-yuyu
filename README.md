# 👋 Hi there, I'm Xu Zhang (张旭)

I am a **Ph.D. candidate at [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/)** and a member of [SIGMA Lab](https://sigma.whu.edu.cn/). My research focuses on **all-in-one image restoration**, **multimodal perception and reasoning**, and **world models**.

🌐 **[Academic Homepage](https://house-yuyu.github.io/)** · 📚 **[Publications](https://house-yuyu.github.io/#publications)** · 📫 **[Email](mailto:zhangx0802@whu.edu.cn)**

I welcome research collaborations. Feel free to get in touch!

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **All-in-One & Agentic Image Restoration** — unified restoration, degradation perception, multimodal reasoning, and decision-making with feedback.
- **Multimodal & Spatial Intelligence** — vision-language models, controllable generation, VLA, and 3D perception.
- **World Models** — predictive representations, interactive environments, embodied intelligence, and autonomous driving.

## 📰 Selected Highlights

- **2026 · IJCAI:** [InterLight](https://github.com/House-yuyu/InterLight), leveraging intrinsic illumination priors for low-light image enhancement.
- **2026 · AAAI:** [ClearAIR](https://github.com/House-yuyu/ClearAIR) and [Any2RSI](https://github.com/House-yuyu/Any2RSI) published; **Any2RSI was selected for an oral presentation**.
- **2025 · IEEE TIP:** [Perceive-IR](https://github.com/House-yuyu/Perceive-IR) and [UniUIR](https://github.com/House-yuyu/UniUIR) published.

More publications and news are available on my [academic homepage](https://house-yuyu.github.io/).
